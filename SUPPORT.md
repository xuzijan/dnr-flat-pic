# Voluntary Support / 自愿支持

DnR FlatPic v2.0 is free and open source. If it saves you time, you may optionally leave a tip. Tipping is never required and does not unlock features, services, support priority, or additional rights.

DnR FlatPic v2.0 免费且开源。如果它为你节省了时间，可以自愿打赏。打赏不是必需条件，也不会解锁功能、服务、优先支持或额外权利。
